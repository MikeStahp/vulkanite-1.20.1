## 2024-05-18 - Replacing Stream operations in Java rendering hot paths
**Learning:** Usage of `Stream` methods (like `.stream().mapToInt(...)` or `.stream().map(...)`) inside hot rendering methods (such as `VulkanPipeline.renderPostShadows`, `VCmdBuff.bindDSet`, `CommandManager.Queue.waitForExecutions`) allocates many temporary objects per frame. This includes Iterator implementations, Lambda classes, and intermediate lists which all put pressure on the Garbage Collector (GC), and eventually triggers expensive pauses, causing stutter.
**Action:** Replace `Stream` calls inside high-frequency, frame-by-frame critical loops with simple enhanced `for` loops or traditional indexed loops, utilizing pre-allocated primitive arrays or localized ArrayLists when needed to completely avoid garbage generation in the per-frame critical path.
