## 2024-05-24 - Stream Usage in Hot Paths\n**Learning:** Java Streams (e.g., `.stream().mapToLong().toArray()`, `.toList()`) used in hot paths like `VCmdBuff.bindDSet` and `CommandManager.waitForExecutions` allocate significant iterator and wrapper object garbage per frame, leading to GC pressure in this specific Minecraft mod architecture.\n**Action:** Refactor these stream operations to traditional `for` loops with pre-sized arrays/collections to eliminate per-frame allocations.
