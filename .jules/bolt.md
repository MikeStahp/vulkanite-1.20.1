## 2024-05-24 - Avoid streams and lambdas in hot paths
**Learning:** Using `java.util.stream` (e.g., `stream().mapToLong().max()`, `stream().map().toList()`) and lambda-based collection methods (e.g., `Map.compute`, `Map.forEach`) creates substantial garbage collection pressure in per-frame rendering code like Vulkanite. Additionally, `executions.stream().mapToLong(Long::longValue).max().orElse(waitingFor.getOrDefault(execQueue, 0))` caused a subtle bug where the timeline semaphore max could incorrectly decrease if the incoming list max was lower than the current wait value.
**Action:** Always replace streams and closures in hot rendering paths with traditional `for` loops. Specifically use `Map.get`/`Map.put` instead of `Map.compute`, and manually track maximums in loops to ensure monotonically increasing timeline semaphores.
