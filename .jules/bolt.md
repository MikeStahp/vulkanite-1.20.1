## 2024-05-19 - [Avoid Capturing Lambdas in Hot Rendering Paths]
**Learning:** In Java, using lambdas that capture local variables (like `builderMap.forEach((layer,buffer)->{ ... buffers.add(...) })`) causes a new object allocation every single time the method is executed. When these methods are called per-frame in a rendering loop (like `EntityCapture.end()`), this creates continuous garbage collection pressure, negatively impacting frame times and consistency. Additionally, `Map.compute` methods can also incur unintended overhead (like `Map.Entry` allocations or lambda instantiations) compared to traditional explicit `get`/`put` operations.
**Action:** When optimizing hot paths executed every frame, systematically search for and replace capturing lambdas (`.forEach()`, `.compute()`) with traditional indexed `for` loops or enhanced `for` loops over `entrySet()`. Ensure early `return` statements inside lambdas are explicitly translated to `continue` statements in the standard loops to preserve logic integrity.
