## 2024-06-25 - Streams vs. Loops in Hot Rendering Paths
**Learning:** In highly performance-critical, per-frame execution paths (such as `VulkanPipeline.renderPostShadows`, `VCmdBuff.bindDSet`, `CommandManager.Queue.waitForExecutions`, and `MixinIrisRenderingPipeline.getCustomTextures`), utilizing Java Streams inherently leads to noticeable CPU overhead through per-frame GC allocations for lambda expressions, wrapper types, and the iterators underpinning the streams. Notably, operations relying on Unboxing overhead, such as `mapToInt`, further decrease efficiency.
**Action:** When working in these hot loops within the `me.cortex.vulkanite` architecture, actively replace stream constructs with rudimentary indexed loops and explicit pre-allocated arrays (or explicit-sized collections) to optimize away dynamic allocation and bypass wrapper allocations to keep execution overhead minimal. Always comment *why* you are substituting arrays or explicit `for` loop iteration logic to preserve readability.
