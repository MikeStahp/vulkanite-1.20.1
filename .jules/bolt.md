## 2024-05-18 - Optimize chunk upload path by avoiding ArrayList allocation
**Learning:** In the chunk upload hot path (`MixinRenderSectionManager.processResults`), a map of `ChunkBuildOutput` objects (`map.values()`) was being passed to `Vulkanite.upload()`. However, `Vulkanite.upload()` and its delegates (`AccelerationManager.chunkBuilds()`, `AccelerationBlasBuilder.enqueue()`, and `BLASJobEnqueuer.enqueue()`) strictly required a `List<ChunkBuildOutput>`. This forced `MixinRenderSectionManager` to wrap the `map.values()` collection in a `new ArrayList<>(map.values())`, causing an unnecessary $O(N)$ object allocation and copy on every update tick.
**Action:** Changed the signature of `upload`, `chunkBuilds`, and `enqueue` to accept `Collection<ChunkBuildOutput>` instead of `List<ChunkBuildOutput>`, allowing `map.values()` to be passed directly without allocating an intermediary array list. This prevents redundant object creation and array copying during chunk rendering loops.
